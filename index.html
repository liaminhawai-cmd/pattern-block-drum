How the Pattern Block Drum Machine works

The fraction wall at the top is your bin of wooden blocks. Every block is a slice of one bar: a whole 1, a 1/2, a 1/3, all the way to 1/12.

  • Build a rhythm — drag a block from the wall into a lane. Blocks tile from the left, and a block's width is its length in time.
  • Rotate the faces — click a placed block to turn it to its next face: loud → mid → soft → mute. That sets how hard it hits. A muted block still holds its time — so it works as a rest.
  • Subdivide a beat — right-click a block (or hover and press 2–6). Cut a 1/5 into 3 and you get three 1/15 pieces. Press M to merge a block back into the one after it.
  • Bring your own sounds — each lane has its own sampler. Drag an audio file onto a lane, or click 📁. It ships with built-in drum voices so it plays right away.
  • Mix — M mutes a lane, S solos it, the slider sets its level.

Everything runs locally in your browser with the Web Audio API. Nothing is uploaded.
